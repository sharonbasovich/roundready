What this does, and what it cannot promise

A paper stock card or spreadsheet can do this arithmetic. This prototype makes cupboard limits, missing checks and product-by-product shortages visible together. It cannot verify stock condition, product suitability, physical delivery, access or real demand. Follow your organization’s storage and product-specific safety procedures.

Usage ranges are optional operator assumptions in whole units per day. They are not forecasts. Enter no identities, precise private locations, medical details or individual usage. Synthetic Station A/B/C labels are intentional.
